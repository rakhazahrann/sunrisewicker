## Brand

- Brand: SUNRISE(TM)
- Manufacturer: PT. Karya Mandiri Indoplast
- Product: high-quality Polyethylene (PE) synthetic wicker
- Applications: outdoor furniture and indoor furniture
- Contact: sunrisewicker@gmail.com / +62 811-30939
- Address: Kawasan Industri Dan Pergudangan SIRIE Blok S-12, Jl. Lingkar Timur KM 5, Rangkah Kidul, Sidoarjo, Jawa Timur

## Product Properties

- Highly durable
- Resistant to harsh weather and temperature differences
- Environmentally friendly
- 100% recyclable
- Manufactured using extrusion technology
- In-house color compounding
- Antioxidant formulation
- Light stabilizer for protection against UV rays

## Normalization Rules

- Correct obvious typographical errors in website display, such as `WHTIE` to `WHITE`, `SIMMER NICKEL` to `SHIMMER NICKEL`, and `67MM` to `7 MM`.
- When spreadsheet dimensions conflict with product names or image filenames, use the product name and matching image filename.
- Preserve distinctive commercial color names even when spelling is unusual.
- Do not invent certifications, warranty periods, test results, or performance figures absent from source material.
